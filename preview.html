EXPORT PRODUCT PORTFOLIO

We Specialize in Exporting 9 Premium Lines

Carefully sourced, rigorously inspected, and packaged for worldwide ocean and air delivery.

1) Readymade Garments

Export-grade men's, women's, and children's readymade apparel crafted from premium cotton, blends, and fine textiles.

2) Bed Sheets

Luxury hotel collection and domestic bed sheets, pillow covers, and bed spreads in high thread count pure cotton & satin.

3) Clothing Materials

Unstitched fabric rolls, organic cotton yards, printed textiles, and bespoke woven dress materials.

4) Packed Snacks

Authentic Kerala banana chips fried in pure coconut oil, spiced tapioca crisps, and traditional savory namkeens.

5) Fruits (incl. Jackfruit)

Fresh and processed tropical Kerala fruits, with special focus on premium farm-harvested Jackfruit bulbs and slices.

6) Cereals

Finest aged Basmati rice, traditional Kerala Palakkadan Matta rice, non-basmati varieties, and high-nutrition millets.

7) Pulses

Machine-cleaned yellow toor dal, chickpeas (chana), moong dal, red lentils, and kidney beans graded for export.

8) Coconut Oil

100% pure cold-pressed expeller and virgin coconut oil extracted from authentic Kerala sun-dried copra coconuts.

9) Spices

Malabar Tellicherry black pepper, green cardamom pods, cloves, cinnamon, and golden turmeric from Kerala's spice hills.
